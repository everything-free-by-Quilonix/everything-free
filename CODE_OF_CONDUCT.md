# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Everything.Free to be a project people are glad they contributed to, regardless of experience, background, identity or circumstances.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, and to act in ways that make this a welcoming and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, and extend it before assuming otherwise
- Give feedback on the work, not the person
- Accept that others may know something you do not, and that you may know something they do not
- Be clear about what you are certain of and what you are guessing — this matters especially here, where accuracy is the product
- Take responsibility for mistakes, correct them, and move on
- Remember that contributors are volunteers spending time they did not owe anyone

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Insults, personal attacks, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Deliberately submitting false information about a resource's free status, licensing or limitations
- Using the project to promote a commercial interest without disclosing it

The last two are specific to this project. The library is only useful if its entries are accurate, so knowingly misrepresenting a resource is treated as a conduct issue, not merely a mistake.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and code review — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately through a [GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/everything-free-by-Quilonix/everything-free/security/advisories/new), which is not publicly visible. Reports will be reviewed promptly and the reporter's privacy respected.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take any action they judge appropriate, including:

1. **Correction** — a private explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate
2. **Warning** — consequences stated for continued behaviour
3. **Temporary ban** — from interaction with the project for a specified period
4. **Permanent ban** — from any public interaction with the project

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face repercussions determined by the project's leadership.
